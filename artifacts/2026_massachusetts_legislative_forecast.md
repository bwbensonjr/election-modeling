## Change from the 60-day snapshot

The 60-day snapshot, frozen at the 2026-09-04 finance cutoff, averaged 31.0 Democratic wins; the 14-day snapshot averages 31.3. The average race margin moved 1.6 points. The largest moves were 10th Bristol (+43.8 to +24.7), 1st Bristol and Plymouth (+14.9 to +20.1), and 35th Middlesex (+55.8 to +60.0). The 10th Bristol move follows the opponent's receipts rising from zero to $3,950, which sharply narrows a log receipt ratio that began at an extreme. Both snapshots remain published unchanged.

## Model and interpretation

The forecast uses a Bayesian Gaussian regression of Democratic margin on district PVI, incumbency, and the log ratio of Democratic to opponent receipts measured 14 days before the election, over the trailing 365 days. It was trained on eligible completed races through November 5, 2024.

Race intervals include coefficient uncertainty and residual race-level variation. Chamber draws preserve dependence represented by shared model parameters, but they do not add a shared 2026 statewide election shock.

Among the 39 listed House contests, the median is 25 Democratic wins with a 90% range of 22 to 28. Among the seven listed Senate contests, the median is 6 with a 90% range of 5 to 7.
