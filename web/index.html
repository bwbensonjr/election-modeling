What the controls mean

The model associates race outcomes with district partisanship, incumbency, and—where complete—candidate receipts measured at the forecast horizon. Changing fundraising here does not show the causal effect of raising that money; fundraising can itself reflect how competitive a race appears.

The published forecast stays fixed. A scenario applies only your edited inputs to the same paired posterior draws, so differences are not browser resampling noise.
