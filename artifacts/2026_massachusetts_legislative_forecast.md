Competitiveness is measured by closeness to a 50% Democratic win probability. A positive model margin favors the Democrat. Intervals are the 90% posterior predictive range in percentage points.

## Model and interpretation

The forecast uses a Bayesian Gaussian regression of Democratic margin on district PVI, incumbency, and the log ratio of Democratic to opponent receipts measured 60 days before the election. It was trained on eligible completed races through November 5, 2024.

Race intervals include coefficient uncertainty and residual race-level variation. Chamber draws preserve dependence represented by shared model parameters, but they do not add a shared 2026 statewide election shock.

Among the 39 listed House contests, the median is 25 Democratic wins with a 90% range of 22 to 28. Among the seven listed Senate contests, the median is 6 with a 90% range of 5 to 7.
